What types of projects do you typically work on?

I build full-stack web products, cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter, high-performance backends and low-level systems in Rust, real-time communication systems (WebRTC, WebSockets, live chat), developer tools, and AI agent workflows.

How long does a typical project take?

Focused prototypes and landing experiences typically ship in 1 to 3 weeks. Full-stack products and real-time systems usually take 4 to 8 weeks with weekly shippable milestones.

What is your development process like?

You work directly with me—no handoffs or layers. We clarify the problem, build a working interactive interface early, wire up tested backend APIs, and verify across desktop and mobile before launch.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Several platforms in my portfolio—like Exceed Learning Center and Unbreakable You—are long-term partnerships where I handle new features, maintenance, and performance optimization over time.

Can you work with our existing codebase and team?

Absolutely. Whether shipping features inside an existing Python/Flask, Rust, React/TypeScript, or Flutter mobile repository, writing low-level systems and AST linters, or building standalone backend services, I integrate cleanly with your workflow.
